# References

Screenshots that show the look and feel Pool! is after. `docs/STYLE.md` is the rulebook; these are the pictures behind it.

**Reference only, never copy.** Nothing from these images goes into the game: no models, textures, images, UI art, effects, layouts traced from them, or code. Everything in Pool! is made by us, in code, in Studio, or with Studio's AI tools from our own prompts, which never name another game.

## What to add

| Folder | What to capture |
| --- | --- |
| `lodge/` | Cozy lodge and cabin rooms: timber beams, plank floors, lanterns, how warm light pools on wood, plants, seating, a counter. Day and evening light |
| `pool-halls/` | Real pool halls: tables under hanging lamps, how light falls on the felt, where people stand to watch, how the room is laid out around the tables |
| `illegal-soccer/` | The feel of Illegal Soccer: its colors, chunky shapes, matte painted look, cards and buttons, how big moments are shown, and how short its effects are |
| `8-ball-duels/` | The feel of 8 Ball Duels: the top-down view, the aim line, the power meter, the HUD layout, the player cards, the end screen |

## How to add one

- PNG or JPG, in the folder above that fits, named for what it shows, like `lodge/beams-lantern-light.png`.
- Add a line under Files: the file and the one thing to look at in it, like "the warm light on the floor, not the furniture".
- Crop to the part that matters. A few sharp screenshots beat many loose ones.
