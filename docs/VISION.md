# Vision

A one-page draft by Claude, for Daniel to rewrite in Daniel's own words. GAME_SPEC says what the game does; this says why, and what to protect when choices get hard.

## A great match

Two friends walk up to a table in a warm lodge, press E, and three seconds later they're playing. The break cracks and the rack scatters. Every shot feels exact: the line shows where the balls will go, the pull works like a slingshot, and a pot lands with a solid click and a small pop. Halfway through, the player who's behind fills their item bar and freezes the one ball the leader needed, and the table erupts on voice chat. Four minutes later someone calls a corner pocket, sinks the 8, and both of them hit Rematch before the end screen closes.

## Who it's for

Roblox players aged about 10 to 15, and a little older, who play with friends and want something quick, competitive and a bit chaotic. They know what pool is, but not its rules, so the game teaches them on the first join. They play on PC first, and on phones by launch. A game fits in 3 to 5 minutes, so one more game is always easy.

## Three pillars

1. **The shot is exact.** Aiming and physics match the feel of 8 Ball Duels. Balls go where the line says, every time, on every screen. Nothing random decides a shot.
2. **Items make it wild, never unfair.** Gadgets bend the physics but never the rules. Everyone sees what was used and what it does, and the player who's behind still has a way back.
3. **A table is a hangout.** Players walk around, talk, watch and rematch. The lodge is cozy, the games are short, and a table is as much about the people at it as the shot.

## What Pool! is not

- **Not a simulator.** No realistic cloth, no spin for now, and 8-ball only: no 9-ball or snooker.
- **Not a grind.** Progression rewards playing; it never decides who wins a game.
- **Not a copy.** Other games are references for feel only. Everything in Pool! is made for Pool!.
- **Not violent or adult.** No guns, no weapon items, no alcohol.
- **Not a game against bots.** Every opponent in a real match is a real player. The only bot is Coach, who teaches new players in the tutorial.
- **Not settled on money yet.** What, if anything, is for sale is still open; nothing is built for it.
